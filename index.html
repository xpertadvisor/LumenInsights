About Us

Lumen Insights is a boutique advisory firm supporting freelancers and small businesses with financial management and business infrastructure.

We combine professional bookkeeping expertise with modern digital solutions to help businesses operate more efficiently.

Our services include bookkeeping, tax compliance support, corporate secretarial services, business advisory, and the development of custom software tools, websites, and business automation systems that simplify operations and improve financial visibility.

By integrating finance and technology, we help entrepreneurs streamline workflows, gain clearer insights into their numbers, and focus on scaling their business.
