Annual Return Filing

All Singapore-incorporated companies must file an Annual Return with ACRA to update key company details and confirm compliance. Filing must be done within a specified period after the AGM or the company's financial year-end.

CPF Contribution Basics for Employers

In Singapore, the Central Provident Fund (CPF) is a mandatory social security savings scheme for Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents. Employers must make CPF contributions for eligible employees.
